The Dynamics of Global Warming

1. Industrial Forcing

CO2 and Methane (CH4) trap longwave infrared radiation, acting as a greenhouse blanket that forces atmospheric temperatures upward [olGE09].

2. Systemic Ocean Crisis

Rapid thermal absorption causes massive ice sheet decay, leading to accelerated sea-level rise and severe ocean acidification, which destroys marine ecosystems [olGE09].

The Green Initiative Protocols

1. Grid Decarbonization

Urgent transition to 100% renewable energy grids (solar, wind) is mandatory to halt CO2 emissions [olGE09].

2. Ecological Restoration

Mass afforestation, modeled on initiatives like Ethiopia's Green Legacy, restores soil health and creates natural carbon sinks [olGE09].
